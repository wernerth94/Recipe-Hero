## Content of the App
The app uses AI to extract recipes from online webpages and store the locally on your device and summarized HTML pages.

## Monetization 
The first 10 invocations of the LLM model are free (either creating a new recipe or re-generating and existing one).
After that, the app asks for a one-time payment to unlock the premium product, allowing for unlimited invocations of the LLM model.
The app does not include any kind of subscription model.
If premium is not bought, the already created recipes can be viewed and edited manually, but no new recipe can be created.
